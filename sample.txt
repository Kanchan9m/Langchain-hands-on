Today, I want to talk about the power of artificial intelligence.  
AI is not just about robots or futuristic technology—it’s about making our everyday lives easier.  
From personalized recommendations on Netflix to AI assistants like Siri and Alexa, we are already surrounded by AI.  

But AI also comes with responsibilities.  
We must ensure that these systems are built ethically, transparently, and fairly.  
The future is not about AI replacing humans—it’s about AI working with humans to create better opportunities.  

AI in healthcare is revolutionizing diagnosis and treatment.  
Doctors can now use AI tools to detect diseases early, analyze X-rays, and recommend personalized treatments.  
In education, AI can help students learn at their own pace by offering adaptive learning platforms.  
In transportation, self-driving cars are becoming a reality, promising safer and more efficient travel.  

However, we must remain cautious.  
AI systems are only as good as the data we feed them.  
If the data is biased, the outcomes will also be biased.  
That’s why building trustworthy AI is critical for the future.  

Thank you.
